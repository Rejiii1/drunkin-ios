## Description (4000)

Drunkin turns any bourbon, whiskey, wine or beer night into a proper blind-scored tasting.

One person hosts and sets up the flights. Everyone else joins from their own iPhone with a 6-digit code. Score each pour from 1 to 5, add notes, mark if you added water. Scores stay hidden until everyone at the table is in, then reveal on every phone at once, so nobody gets swayed by the loudest palate.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
Phones connect straight to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Cabin in the woods, basement bar, no service: it just works.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No sign-up. No servers. No ads. No tracking. Your tastings live on your phone and the phones of the people at your table, and nowhere else.

AFTER THE LAST POUR
• Pour of the flight, everyone's favorite and the biggest debate
• A leaderboard across every flight
• Awards: toughest grader, softest touch, contrarian, poet laureate
• Your own tasting notes in one place
• Share results to the group chat as text, or send the whole tasting as a file anyone with Drunkin can open

FOR THE HOST
• Build flights and pours with proof, style, nose, palate, finish and a fun fact
• Decide who's at the table for each flight
• Reveal a pour early, or hold it back
• Start from a template: bourbon basics, a blind A-B-C-D flight, or blank
• Hand hosting to another phone mid-tasting

Drunkin is for adults of legal drinking age. Please drink responsibly.
