Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Drunkin is a tasting app that runs entirely on your iPhone. We built it so there is nothing to collect: no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Drunkin does not receive, store or have access to any information about you or your tastings. The app does not contain any third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics code.

What stays on your phone

The tastings you host or join (menus, the names people enter, scores, tasting notes and profile photos you choose) are saved on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection. They are included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on, under your Apple Account, not ours.

What is shared, and with whom

  • Phones at your table. When you host a tasting, nearby phones that enter your 6-digit join code connect directly to yours over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity, encrypted end to end. Nothing passes through the internet or any server. Guests receive the menu, the names and photos at the table, who has scored, every revealed score, and their own entries. A guest never receives another person's score until that pour is revealed.
  • Nearby devices. While a tasting is open for joining, your phone advertises the tasting's name and the host's display name to nearby devices running Drunkin so they can find it.
  • Files you send. If you choose Share tasting file, Drunkin creates a file with the menu, names, photos and revealed scores, and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files) is your choice and is governed by that service. A hand-off file, which you create only to move hosting to another phone, also contains hidden scores.

Photos

If you add a profile photo, you pick it with the iOS photo picker; Drunkin only sees the single photo you choose. It is shrunk to a small square and all metadata, including location, is removed before it is saved or shared with your table.

Permissions

Drunkin asks for Local Network access so it can find and connect to the phones at your table. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or full photo library.

Deleting your data

Delete a single tasting from its screen, or everything from Settings → Delete all Drunkin data on this phone. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Copies on other people's phones are theirs and are not affected.

Age

Drunkin is intended only for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
